## Direction contract

THESIS: A serious contemporary agency site in the register of the user's Pinterest references (Stodio, Novacraft, Creatix): structured grid, alternating dark and light sections, large real imagery, one restrained accent. Refuses themed metaphors and decoration.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black #0E0E0F and white/neutral #F3F3F1 grounds; Mi Alma pink (#D10F72 for UI, logo keeps its own pink) as the only accent, on primary buttons and small markers. General Sans for headings (500/600, tight tracking), Switzer for text. Radius hierarchy 24 panels / 16 images and cards / pill buttons. Hairline dividers.

STORY: Understand in one viewport what Mi Alma does and for whom, see real clients (avatars, then iPhones with their live feeds), the services and the four-step method, meet Gaia, write on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inset dark rounded hero with the B&W behind-the-scenes video under a dark scrim; nav inside the top; headline bottom-left at display scale; at bottom-right a short description plus pink "Guarda i lavori" and white WhatsApp button; a thin row of the four services along the hero's foot. Client avatar strip just below the fold edge.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) "deve essere una cosa seria. ben strutturata, non a caso ... fai qualcosa di serio come gli screen che ho preso da pinterest". Signature interaction: five iPhones with scrollable real screenshots and Profilo/Post toggle. Motion: restrained fades of hero content on load only, hover states on rows and buttons.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
