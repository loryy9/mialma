## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the town's poster board (pubbliche affissioni): local businesses pasted side by side, the analog twin of an Instagram grid. It refuses the dark agency hero with neon accent and the row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Dark galvanised-steel board frames (#3B4441 to #1d2321) holding 70x100 posters glued edge to edge on a concrete-grey wall (#ECEDE8 paper, #15110F ink). Mi Alma pink fluo stock (#F2218A) is the poster that carries actions; acid yellow (#FFE14D) only as rubber date stamps. Condensed heavy grotesk (Archivo, wdth 62) for posters, Schibsted Grotesk for text. Slot numbers stencilled on the frame. Hard paper edges, slight glue offsets, no rounded cards.

STORY: Visitor sees in one look what Mi Alma does (films your business and runs your Instagram), sees five real local clients' feeds on phones, understands the four-step method, and tears off a WhatsApp strip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Frame across full width. Left 7 cols: pink poster, headline "Ti facciamo vedere." huge, subline, tear-off strip row at its foot (primary action, opens WhatsApp). Right 5 cols: white poster with an iPhone showing La Camerona profile, stamped date. Nav as a frame header plate.

FORM: Spazio affissioni, candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list, seed: 40f56c9309c2e7ca. Signature interaction: tear-off strips that peel on hover and open WhatsApp; iPhone screens scroll between profile and grid views. Motion grammar: posters slapped on (short drop + settle), strips peel.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
